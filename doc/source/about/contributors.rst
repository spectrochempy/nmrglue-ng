Project history and contributors
================================

``nmrglue-ng`` is built on the complete history of the original
`nmrglue project <https://github.com/jjhelmus/nmrglue>`_. The project is
grateful to everyone who contributed code, documentation, examples, testing,
reviews, bug reports, and scientific knowledge.

.. important:: Project origins and stewardship

   **Jonathan J. Helmus** conceived and created nmrglue, establishing its
   scientific and technical foundations. **Kaustubh R. Mote** has been his
   principal long-standing collaborator on the project and a leading
   maintainer in recent years, carrying a substantial share of its ongoing
   development and maintenance.

   Their work can be explored through the upstream profiles of
   `Jonathan J. Helmus <https://github.com/jjhelmus>`_ and
   `Kaustubh R. Mote <https://github.com/kaustubhmote>`_.

This page distinguishes work on the independent continuation from the
historical contributions inherited from nmrglue. Git history remains the
authoritative attribution record.
